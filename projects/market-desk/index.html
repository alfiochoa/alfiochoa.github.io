Market Desk

A keyboard-driven market terminal on free public data, with a source badge on every panel.

Open the live dashboardCode on GitHub

A short silent walkthrough. The diagrams are schematic illustrations, labelled as such in the video.

The problem

Free public market data is scattered across APIs that fail, rate-limit or lack CORS. You want one dense dashboard.

What it does

  • Draggable widgets, a command palette and charts with unit-tested indicators (SMA, EMA, Bollinger bands, RSI and MACD).
  • Crypto, FX and Treasury panels load live from free public APIs with a fallback chain and a cache. Every panel carries a LIVE, CACHED, SNAPSHOT or ERROR badge. FRED and news come from hourly GitHub Action snapshots.
  • US equities are Twelve Data end-of-day closes plus delayed intraday quotes for 15 priority symbols. Never real-time.

Honest limits

  • Equity prices are free-tier end-of-day closes plus delayed intraday quotes for 15 priority symbols (best-effort refresh), never real-time.
  • Crypto, FX and Treasury panels depend on free third-party APIs that can be rate-limited. FRED and news update hourly at best.
  • Reference and demo data, not investment advice, and no trading. Not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any data provider.
