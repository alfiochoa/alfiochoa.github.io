S&P 500 Tracker & Analysis

The top 200 S&P 500 stocks by market cap: sector weights, returns, movers, scores and correlations.

Open the live demoCode on GitHub

A short silent walkthrough. The diagrams are schematic illustrations, labelled as such in the video.

The problem

You want sector weights, returns, movers, scores and correlations for a stock universe in one dashboard that always renders.

What it does

  • Sector weights, 1-day, 1-week, 1-month and year-to-date returns, movers, momentum and volatility scores, a correlation heatmap and a watchlist with mocked webhook alerts and CSV export.
  • The ranking uses a documented SEC EDGAR-based market-cap method. Constituents come from Wikipedia, with attribution, robots.txt awareness and a breakage detector.
  • Every symbol has a real, delayed end-of-day close from the Twelve Data free tier, refreshed on a credit budget (stale symbols only).
